# ARC v11 — Exercise Visuals

- Platform logic, plans, timers, syncing and training workflow remain unchanged from v10.
- Replaced the generic/incorrect exercise drawings with a new exercise-specific SVG layer.
- Strength and rehab movements use start/end schematics with exercise-specific equipment and joint positions.
- CrossFit/calisthenics movements have distinct schematics instead of shared generic figures.
- Endurance sessions keep sport-specific visuals while the badge identifies the exact workout type (e.g. Z2, 400 m, CSS, VO2, Sweet Spot).
- Reference basis used for form/position checks: ACE Exercise Library, CrossFit movement demos/training guide, AAOS shoulder conditioning, Mayo Clinic core exercise references, NHS balance guidance.
- Illustrations are original schematics for identification. They are not copied photographs and are not a substitute for technique coaching or clinical assessment.
