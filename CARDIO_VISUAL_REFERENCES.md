# ARC v12 — Cardio visual references

The v12 endurance illustrations are original SVG schematics. They were redrawn to match recognizable sport-specific positions rather than reusing a generic stick figure.

Reference principles used:

- **Running:** Hospital for Special Surgery guidance on relaxed shoulders, roughly 90° elbow bend, tall torso with a slight whole-body forward lean, and landing beneath the center of mass; World Athletics also emphasizes symmetry and relaxed upper-body action.
- **Freestyle swimming:** U.S. Masters Swimming guidance on a long body line, neutral head position, shoulder/hip rotation, and an early vertical forearm/high-elbow catch. Pull-buoy, kick, breathing and open-water variants are visually distinguished.
- **Cycling:** British Cycling guidance on sustainable position, relaxed upper body, appropriate saddle/leg extension and smooth cadence. Climbing, sprint, cadence, torque and brick variants are visually distinguished.
- **Inline skating:** World Skate technical criteria emphasize balance, rhythmic knee action, precise foot placement, glide, edge control, acceleration and one-foot skating. ARC additionally distinguishes one-leg glide, slalom, cornering, T-stop braking, hills and sprinting.
- **Rowing:** Concept2 technique model: catch with arms straight and shins near vertical; drive begins with the legs; finish with legs extended, slight layback and handle at the lower ribs; recovery reverses the sequence.
- **Hiking:** REI guidance for trekking poles and posture on level ground, climbs and descents; loaded hiking is shown with a pack rather than as generic walking.

These drawings are identification aids, not a substitute for coaching, medical advice or a full technique lesson.
