# ARC v11 — Visual reference basis

The exercise illustrations in ARC v11 are original schematic SVG drawings. They were redrawn from scratch and are intended to help users identify an exercise. They are not copies of third-party photos or illustrations.

Reference basis used to check body position, equipment orientation and start/end positions:

- ACE Exercise Library — broad strength/bodyweight/machine exercise library with form descriptions and photos: https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/
- CrossFit Exercise Demos / Level 1 movement guide — air squat, press, push press, deadlift, thruster, wall ball, pull-up, muscle-up, snatch and related functional movements: https://exercisedemos.crossfit.com/workout/
- CrossFit Air Squat: https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/essential-air-squat
- CrossFit Deadlift: https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/the-deadlift-crossfit-foundational-movement
- CrossFit Thruster: https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/the-thruster
- CrossFit Push Press: https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/the-push-press
- AAOS Rotator Cuff and Shoulder Conditioning Program — internal/external rotation reference positions: https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/globalassets/pdfs/2024-rotator-cuff-and-shoulder-conditioning-program.pdf
- Mayo Clinic core exercise references — bridge, quadruped/bird dog, side plank and related core positions: https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness/in-depth/core-strength/art-20546851
- NHS balance exercises — one-leg balance and controlled balance positions: https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/balance-exercises/

ARC uses simplified line drawings because they remain legible in small cards and scale cleanly in the full-screen training view. Technique cues and clinical decisions should not be inferred from a tiny icon alone.
